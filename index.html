How to play

Split each zone into rectangles. Every rectangle holds exactly one number, and that number is its area.

4
2

Drag across cells to draw a rectangle. Tap a rectangle to remove it. Tap a 1 to fill it.

Solve a zone to unlock its neighbours, then hit Continue to glide to the next one. The world never ends, and it gets harder the further you travel.

Your clock starts with your first rectangle and pauses whenever you look away or zoom out. Stuck? Tap Easier for a smaller Shikaku nearby.

The gold house marks where you started; the house button takes you back. Zoom with + / − or fit the view with ⤢.

Drag on empty space to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Pause (Esc) shows your stats. Progress saves automatically on this device, separately for each mode.
