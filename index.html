How to play

Split each zone into rectangles. Every rectangle holds exactly one number, and that number is its area.

4
2

Drag across cells to draw a rectangle. Tap a rectangle to remove it. Tap a 1 to fill it.

Solve a zone to unlock its neighbours, then hit Continue to glide to the next one. The world never ends, and it gets harder the further you travel.

The deeper you go, the bigger the zones: 6×6, 8×8, 10×10, 12×12, 15×15, 16×16, 18×18, 20×20, then 24×24. Where the size steps up, a zone unlocks only when every zone along one side is solved, plus one diagonal zone past that side's corner.

Use + / − / fit to zoom. If a zone is too tough, tap Easier to jump to the nearest smaller grid.

Drag on empty space to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Progress saves automatically on this device.
